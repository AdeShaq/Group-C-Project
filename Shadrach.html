My name is Shadrach

Shadrach is a character from the Bible, specifically from the Book of Daniel.

He is one of the three Hebrew youths who were thrown into a fiery furnace by King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon for refusing to worship an image of gold. Shadrach, along with Meshach and Abednego, was miraculously unharmed by the flames, which is seen as a testament to their faith in God.
